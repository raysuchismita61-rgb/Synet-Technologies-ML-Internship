# Task 5: Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Superstore Sales Dataset** to understand business performance, sales trends, and profitability.

The analysis focuses on identifying monthly revenue trends, top-performing products and categories, and overall business insights using Python and data visualization.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective is to analyze sales data and identify important patterns that can help understand business performance.

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information about:

* Order Date
* Ship Date
* Customer
* Product
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Sales
* Quantity
* Discount
* Profit
* Region
* State
* City

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook / VS Code

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trends

Sales data is grouped by month to identify changes in revenue over time.

### 2. Top-Selling Products

The products generating the highest sales are identified and analyzed.

### 3. Category Performance

Different product categories are compared based on:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Quantity Sold

### 4. Regional Performance

Sales and profit are analyzed across different regions to identify the strongest and weakest-performing areas.

### 5. Profitability Analysis

The relationship between sales, discount, and profit is analyzed to understand factors affecting business profitability.

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes visualizations such as:

* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Region

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Monthly sales and revenue patterns
* Best-selling products
* Most profitable categories
* High-performing regions
* Areas with lower profitability
* The impact of discounts on profit
